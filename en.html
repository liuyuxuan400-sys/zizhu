Qingdao student aid, explained simply

Find the student aid your family may qualify for

Qingdao offers fee waivers, grants and living allowances from kindergarten to senior high and vocational school. Many families who qualify never apply because the rules are hard to read. This site turns them into three simple questions.

Good to know

Who this site is for

  • Families in Qingdao with children in kindergarten, primary, junior high, senior high or vocational school.
  • Families on the minimum living allowance (dibao), receiving extreme-poverty support, or recently lifted out of poverty.
  • Families of orphans, children with disabilities, children without de facto caregivers, and children of martyrs.
  • Any family facing financial hardship, such as a serious illness or a sudden crisis.

Beware of scams

You apply for aid through your school. If a call or text claims to be from an aid office and asks you to transfer money or share a bank card password, hang up, then call the school or the hotline to check. Applying for aid never costs money.

    Glossary

    Terms used in the policies
    Dibao householdA family receiving the government's minimum living allowance (最低生活保障).
    Extreme-poverty supportGovernment support for people with no ability to work, no income and no one obliged to support them (特困供养).
    Lifted out of poverty, still supportedA household that has left poverty but is still covered by support policies (脱贫享受政策家庭).
    Monitored for poverty riskA household the government monitors because it is at risk of falling back into poverty (防返贫监测对象).
    Child without de facto caregiversA child whose parents cannot actually raise them, for example because of death, disappearance, serious illness or imprisonment (事实无人抚养儿童).
    Financial hardship recognitionThe school's process for confirming a student comes from a family with financial difficulty (家庭经济困难学生认定). Many grants require it.
    Inclusive kindergartenA public kindergarten, or a private one approved to charge regulated, affordable fees (普惠性幼儿园).
    TierGrants paid at two levels. The school decides the tier based on how much difficulty the family faces.

    How to apply

    From finding aid to getting it

    Except for a few programs such as student-origin credit loans, all student aid is applied for at your child's school or kindergarten after the term starts. Always follow your school's notice.

    The four steps

    1. 1

      Check what you may get

      Use the aid finder. Note which programs say "Needs school approval".

    2. 2

      Prepare documents early

      Gather papers that show your family's situation, such as a dibao certificate or disability certificate.

    3. 3

      Apply at school after term starts

      Watch for the school's aid notice. Submit what it asks for, by its deadline.

    4. 4

      School confirms and pays by tier

      For grants that need approval, the school first confirms financial hardship, then pays at the tier it decides.
