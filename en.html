How to apply

  • Except for a few programs such as student-origin credit loans, all student aid is applied for at your child's school or kindergarten after the term starts.
  • Prepare documents that show your family's situation in advance (such as a dibao certificate or disability certificate). Follow your school's notice for exactly what to submit.
  • Programs marked "Needs school approval" require the school to first confirm the student as being from a family with financial hardship. Aid is then paid by tier.

Questions? Call the hotline

Qingdao Municipal Education Bureau summer student aid hotline. In 2026 it was staffed on weekdays from July 1 to September 15 (9:00–11:30 and 13:30–17:00), and that period has ended. At other times, ask your child's school first.

Each district also has its own aid hotline. Ask your child's school for the number.

Beware of scams

You apply for aid through your school. If a call or text claims to be from an aid office and asks you to transfer money or share a bank card password, hang up, then call the school or the hotline above to check.
